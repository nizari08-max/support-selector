Notes & Assumptions

  • Pipe sizes per ASME B36.10M-2000 (DN 15 to DN 1000).
  • Flange ODs per ASME B16.5-2009 (DN 15 to DN 600) and ASME B16.47-2017 (DN 650 to DN 1000).
  • Adjacent flanges are assumed to be staggered (alternating sides of the support beam).
  • Pipe thermal expansion effects are not included.
  • Minimum centerline spacing: 250 mm (DN ≤ 200) / 350 mm (DN > 200).
  • All values rounded to nearest 5 mm.
  • Reference Excel methodology by Boutrih Boujemaa, April 2024.
