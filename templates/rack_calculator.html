Notes & Assumptions

  • Pipe sizes per ASME B36.10M-2000 (DN 15 to DN 1000).
  • Flange ODs per ASME B16.5-2009 (DN 15 to DN 600) and ASME B16.47-2017 Series A & B (DN 650 to DN 1000).
  • Adjacent flanges are assumed to be staggered (alternating sides of the support beam).
  • Flange @ Section: set per line. The flange envelope is included in the spacing only for pipes that have a flange at the considered rack section; lines set to No use the bare pipe OD.
  • Support: Direct Rest shows the pipe resting on the rack beam; Pipe Shoe shows a nominal schematic shoe (~100 mm). Representation only — it does not change the rack width.
  • Pipe thermal expansion effects are not included.
  • All values rounded to nearest 5 mm.
